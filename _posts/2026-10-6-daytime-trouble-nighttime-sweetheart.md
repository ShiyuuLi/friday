# Daytime Trouble, Nighttime Sweetheart

Friday has a very specific arrangement with me: she gets to be a menace during the day, and I have to forgive her at night.

During the day, she has important things to do. Like playing with her water bowl. Or making sure I never, ever close the bathroom door. Sometimes I feel like I spend half my day negotiating with a tiny creature who doesn't understand the word no.

I can't say she has a very predictable daytime schedule. But her nighttime sleeping spot is always the same.

## Our bedtime routine

Every night, Friday sleeps right next to my pillow.
And somehow, we've developed the habit of holding hands while we sleep. Sometimes I reach my hand toward her, and she rests her little head on it, as though I've thoughtfully provided her with a pillow.

![Friday sleeping by my pillow](assets/img/posts/2026-10-6/pillow.jpg)
_Friday's favorite nighttime spot._


It's honestly ridiculous how quickly I forget all the trouble she caused during the day.
A few hours earlier, I might have been begging her to leave the water bowl alone. Now she's curled up beside me, holding my hand, and I can't imagine being mad at her.
